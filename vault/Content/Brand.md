I turn hard technical skills into simple systems, and show the reps it takes to master them.

## Signature framework: Rep-Stacking
1. **Isolate the variable.** Fix one thing at a time: one joint angle, one function, one hook.
2. **Measure the feedback loop.** When you slip, check the variable, don't blame the wall.
3. **Stack quality volume.** 50 intentional reps daily beat 300 careless ones once a month.

Tagline: "Isolate the variable, execute the rep, measure the feedback, repeat."

## Video structure (the 5-step workflow)
1. **Hook (0 to 8 s):** a high-friction pain point or counter-intuitive truth. No "hey guys".
2. **Reframe (8 to 20 s):** flip the view with simple logic, math or an unexpected angle.
3. **Story and value loop (20 to 50 s):** one real story asset, then 2 to 3 actionable steps.
4. **Delivery:** the Vinh Giang protocol (below).
5. **CTA (50 to 60 s):** one low-friction ask. "Comment REPS" or "Subscribe for daily breakdowns on systems and skill acquisition."

## Delivery protocol (Vinh Giang)
- 1 second of silence, eyes locked on the lens, before the first word.
- Speak at about 80% of normal speed.
- 2-second power pauses replace filler words.
- One continuous take, chest resonance, no over-editing.

## Voice rules (apply to every outline, caption and post)
- Short, punchy, declarative sentences.
- No em dashes.
- Never use "genuinely" or "truly" (use "really").
- No "it's not just..." constructions.
- No "not X but Y" framing.
- Outlines, never word-for-word scripts. I speak from bullets.
