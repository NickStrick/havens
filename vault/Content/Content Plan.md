# Content Plan

## Goal for the next 12 months
Post every day, get measurably better at delivery, and build an audience around the 3 pillars in [[Brand]]. Followers are the lagging metric. Reps and retention are the leading ones.

## Cadence
| Format | Frequency | Where | Time box |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Short (under 60 s, one take) | Daily | YouTube Shorts + Instagram Reels | 20 min record, 10 min post |
| LinkedIn text post (same idea as the short) | 3x per week | LinkedIn | 10 min |
| Long-form whiteboard breakdown (8 to 12 min) | Every 2 weeks | YouTube | 2 hours, weekends |

One idea, three formats. The daily short is the rep. LinkedIn and long-form reuse the same idea.

## Weekly pillar rotation
| Day | Pillar | Series |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mon | 1. Systems | Whiteboard Breakdown: one system made simple |
| Tue | 2. Communication | Explain It Simply: one technical idea for a non-technical listener |
| Wed | 3. Rep-Stacking | The Rep Stack: one skill-acquisition lesson |
| Thu | 2. Communication | SE Lessons: what I learned this week as a new SE (generic, no customers) |
| Fri | 1. Systems | Building Ellora: build log of the agent brigade |
| Sat | 3. Rep-Stacking | Practice field: golf, climbing, discipline, 5 AM |
| Sun | Rest and batch | Review last week's numbers, pick next week's ideas |

## Series
- **Becoming the Best SE:** a public learning journey. Concepts, demo skills, communication lessons. Always generic.
- **Building Ellora:** each weekend's build becomes a video. Pillar 1 and good proof of technical leadership.
- **The Rep Stack:** the skill-acquisition framework applied to a new skill each month. This becomes book material.
- **Whiteboard Breakdowns:** Excalidraw diagrams of systems, explained in 60 seconds.

## Pipeline (folders)
1. `Content/Ideas/`: raw ideas, from me, the Scout's weekly report and Suri's Rep Review
2. `Content/Outlines/`: Kael writes tomorrow's outline every night at 8 pm
3. Record: read the outline twice, breathe, one take
4. `Content/Published/`: one note per published video (template below)
5. Sunday: review the week, feed what worked back into Ideas

## Published video note (template)
```markdown
---
date: {{date:YYYY-MM-DD}}
pillar: 1 | 2 | 3
series:
platforms: [youtube, instagram, linkedin]
hook: ""
views_48h:
retention_pct:      # YouTube average percentage viewed
subs_gained:
saves_shares:
---
## What I tested (one variable)
## What happened
## Keep or change next time
```

Treat each video as a rep: test one variable at a time (the hook, pace, pauses, length, or a title style) and record it in "What I tested".

## Research loop
Every Monday the Scout pulls stats from my favorite creators and niche searches, finds the outlier videos, and writes `Content/Research/` reports plus 5 adapted ideas in `Content/Ideas/`. Kael pulls from Ideas first.

Creators I study: Vinh Giang (delivery), Alexanderthecreate, Alex Hormozi (hooks, offers, reframes), Shi Heng Yi (calm mindset), Jimmy Carr (timing, one-liners).
