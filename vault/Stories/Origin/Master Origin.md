#### Step 1: The "3-Depth Comment" Infiltration Loop (Reaching Mentors Online)

_Do not send dry cold DMs asking "Will you be my mentor?"_ High-value mentors ignore generic requests. Use the **3-Depth Comment Method** on LinkedIn and YouTube:

1. Find 5 to 10 Senior SEs, Pre-Sales Directors, or Keynote Speakers who are 1 to 2 steps ahead of where you want to be.
    
2. Leave insightful, value-add comments on their posts 3 times a week (e.g., re-framing their technical point or offering a specific data point from your 1,000,000-line codebase experience).
    
3. After 2 weeks of consistent comments, send a low-friction DM:
    
    > _"Hey [Name], loved your breakdown on enterprise discovery loops. I applied your insight on my recent live demo rep and it completely shifted the conversation. Appreciate the value you post daily."_
    

#### Step 2: In-Person Event Strategy (Chicago Stage & Community Reps)

1. **Local Meetups (Chicago JS, Midwest SE Network, Cloud Computing Meetups):** Attend in person. Submit 5-minute "Lightning Talks" on _"Translating 1,000,000-Line Codebase Friction into Boardroom Business Margin."_
    
2. **In-Person Toastmasters (Downtown/Loop Chicago):** Move out of online meetings to practice physical stage posture, eye-contact loops, and vocal mechanics in front of live human eyes.
    
3. **The Post-Event Connection:** After someone gives a great presentation, walk up to them immediately and say: _"Your point on handling enterprise buyer objections was spot on. I'm an engineer pivoting into pre-sales—can I buy you a coffee or drop you a quick note on LinkedIn to stay connected?"_
    

#### Step 3: Upgrading Mentors Gracefully

When you reach the income or skill level of a Tier 1 mentor, you do not "dump" them. You transition them into **lifelong peers and friends**, while opening space to seek Tier 2 and Tier 3 mentors who challenge you at your new baseline.
