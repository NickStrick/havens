# Stricker Digital (parked)

**Status:** parked while I master the SE role and build the personal brand. No bender works on this. It lives here so the ideas and lessons aren't lost.

## What it was
Web and marketing services for local businesses in Chicago.
- First niche: local florists and gift shops.
- Niche scoring later favored trades (HVAC, plumbing, roofing) for near-term revenue, and coaches and consultants for ceiling potential.
- Offer ladder: free 10-minute website review, $400 Conversion Upgrade, $2,000 Revenue-Ready Website System. Add-ons: Local SEO, AI Customer Support Assistant.
- Built my own website builder with an editor. No accounts or subscriptions yet, so each client needed a custom implementation, which made it hard to sell at scale.

## What I did and learned
- 2,000 to 3,000 cold calls at 50 dials a day, 3 days a week. Booked in-person and online meetings straight off the first call.
- Wrote call scripts from frameworks. Tiered and prioritized my account list.
- Used AI for lead scraping, qualifying and email automation while keeping a personal touch.
- Lesson: building automation can quietly replace doing outreach. Outreach wins.
- These lessons now feed Pillar 2 (communication) stories and SE prospecting.

## The future vision: Architect-as-a-Service
The fractional CTO and Chief Architect for non-technical founders who need enterprise-level technical clarity without a $250k executive. It follows the Aikido model: diagnose first, then remediate.

| Tier | What | Price | Deliverable |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Technical Diagnosis | 90-minute audit of the whole stack: site, store, databases, APIs, automations, SaaS tools | $1,500 to $2,500 | Whiteboard system map of today vs the simplified future architecture |
| 2. Transformation Sprint | 30-day build as their architect | $5,000 to $10,000 | Stack consolidation, an AI and automation layer, a founder dashboard |

## Product path
1. **High-ticket service:** 20 to 30 audits. Find the 3 or 4 problems every small business has.
2. **Productized workflows:** reusable boilerplates, webhooks and AI templates. Deliver a $10k sprint in 10 hours instead of 40.
3. **SaaS ("Aikido for small-business ops"):** a founder connects their stack, the platform scans for broken data flows, redundant tools and security gaps, and offers one-click AI fixes.
